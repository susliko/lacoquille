## How it forms

**venir** in the present + **de** + the infinitive of the main verb. Before a vowel infinitive (être, aller, avoir), de becomes d'.

## When to use it

An action that has just happened — the mirror of futur proche, pointing backwards instead of forwards. Spoken French leans on it heavily where English uses *have just*.
