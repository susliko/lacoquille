Conjugation drills, compared

Each prototype is a working interactive sketch. Built with the project's design tokens, real conjugation data, and keyboard-first interactions. Try them in order — the first three are the same exercise (cloze) with three different feedback philosophies, so you can compare them directly.

Designed around these principles

  • Retrieval beats re-reading — make them produce, not pick
  • Interleaving transfers better than blocking (feels harder)
  • ~80% success is the sweet spot for learning
  • Forms learned in sentences transfer to real use
  • Three-state feedback: correct / almost (typo, missing accent) / wrong
  • Failed items come back soon, then at longer intervals
  • Show progress per verb & tense, not just a streak
  • Keyboard first — Enter submits, typing is the point
  • Laws of UX applied: Hick, Fitts, Doherty, Von Restorff, Aesthetic-Usability, Peak-End, Jakob's, Law of Proximity
