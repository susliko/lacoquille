Rapid-fire

Sixty seconds. As many cue-to-form items as you can. The clock is the teacher — accuracy still comes first, but speed will follow.

How it works

  • Three chips: verb · person · tense
  • Type the conjugated form, hit Enter
  • Correct → next item, no pause
  • Wrong → brief flash of the correct form, then continue
  • Almost (missing accent) → counted, no streak break
  • Wrong breaks your streak. Build a new one.
Rapid-fire builds automatization — the form comes out before you think.
